Questions people ask

How many guests can the hall hold?

Seated capacity is about 300 guests. Floating capacity (standing receptions) is about 450.

Is catering included?

In-house catering is available, with vegetarian menus starting around ₹500 per plate. Ask about non-veg options and outside caterers when you enquire.

Can I bring my own decorator?

Yes. Both in-house and outside decorators are allowed.

Is there parking?

Yes, the hall has parking space. Tell us how many vehicles you expect so we can plan for it.

Is there a room for the bride?

Yes, there is a dedicated bridal room.

What if the power goes out?

The hall has power backup, so lights, sound and AC keep running.

How do I confirm a booking?

Send your enquiry here or call. We check the date, share a quote, and the date is held once the booking amount is paid.
